The Floor Plan

Five floor plans, 1957–1961

In five buildings, over five years, a body is found five times. Five times the police draw a floor plan, question everyone present and lay out the counters. And five times the same letter turns up in the margin: N.

Every file is a logic puzzle on the floor plan itself: each person in a row and column of their own, the victim alone with his murderer, the weapon within reach. The statements contain exactly enough — and nothing more. Solve them in order: each case sharpens the tools for the next, and the last one gives the letter its name.
