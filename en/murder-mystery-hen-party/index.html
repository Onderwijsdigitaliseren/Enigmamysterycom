Hen & stag

How it works

A murder walk is a detective game you play on foot. You start wherever you like, and the map on your phone places eight leads at real places nearby. At every lead part of the case opens: a witness, a piece of evidence, a camera still.

A perfect fit for a hen party is Till Death Do Us Part: a bride who was due to marry today but never arrived. Or have a case made to measure, with photos of the group, your names in the story and the bride or groom as the lead.

One € 9.95 ticket covers the whole team. Everyone enters the same code, on one phone or each on their own. A case takes about ninety minutes, leaving plenty of time for the rest of the party.

Questions
What does it cost?

One ticket of € 9.95 per team. A personalised version with photos, names and a theme costs € 29.95 including the ticket.

Which towns does it work in?

Anywhere. The map places the leads around the spot where you start, in a city or a village.

How far ahead should I order a custom version?

Request a personalised case at least two weeks ahead, so there is time to prepare it.

Can two groups compete?

Yes, in the Murder Walk. Choose 'teams against each other' in the group case: every team gets its own leads, and you have to trade lead codes, honestly or not.
