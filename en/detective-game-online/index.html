How it works

At Enigma Mystery you play a detective game right in your browser. Nothing to download, no account to make. Open a case and you are at the desk.

There are four kinds of cases. In the story cases you question suspects and file a charge. In the escape rooms you look around in 3D and crack locks. The logic puzzles are pen-and-paper puzzles on screen, and the cozy games play at your own pace, with no clock.

Every case is fictional and has exactly one solution. At least one case of every kind is free. To play everything, buy the Pro pass once for €3.99.

Questions
Is it really free?

Yes, at least one case of every kind is free to play. The other cases are unlocked with the Pro pass, €3.99 once.

Do I need an app?

No. Everything runs in the browser of your phone, tablet or computer. No account needed.

How long does a case take?

From twenty minutes to a few evenings, depending on the case. Your progress is saved on your device.

Can we play together?

Yes. Many people play together on one screen and argue about who did it.
