How it works

Three steps, one killer

  1. Pick a story

    Start with the free trial walk or choose a Pro story. The app uses ordinary places around you: a church, a bank, a hotel.

  2. Walk and collect

    Shadow figures with information appear on the map. Walk up to them, find the evidence, solve the mini-puzzles and question the suspects.

  3. Name the killer

    After about ninety minutes you have enough. One suspect is lying. Does your charge hold?
